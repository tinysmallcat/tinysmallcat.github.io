Stitch Run Labeller

Upload a colourwork chart and every run of consecutive same-colour stitches at or above your threshold gets numbered on its first stitch. No more peering at teeny boxes.

Step 1 — Chart format

SVG is best SVG should give pretty reliable results. Charting software - like Stitchmaster or Stitchfiddle - lets you export as SVG.
Very much a WIP Raster images - your PNGs and JPGs - are far less reliable than SVG here. If you need to use one, PNG is better than JPG for this purpose - but please check the result against the chart you uploaded before hitting the needles.
