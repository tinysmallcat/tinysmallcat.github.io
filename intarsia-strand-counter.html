    How it counts

    • Strand means one length of yarn, wound on its own bobbin. Each row is split into runs of the same colour, worked in knitting order.
    • Carrying: when a colour reappears in the same row after no more than your carry limit, the yarn is stranded behind those stitches and stays the same strand.
    • Following up the rows: a strand continues into the next row when that colour's run there sits on or beside the run below (within the carry limit). Where an area splits, the side nearest the hanging yarn keeps it and the other side gets a new strand. Where two areas join, one strand carries on and the other is cut.
    • Rows per strand counts the rows that strand is knitted in. Colour areas are connected patches of one colour, with the rows they span.
    • Squares at the very edges of shaped pieces can be marked "not a stitch" in the colour list so they aren't counted.
